ABOUT THE PARTY
The Aam Cockroach Party (ACP) is a grassroots political movement built on the principles of honesty, discipline, cleanliness, and justice. Just as the cockroach survives every adversity — nuclear blasts, floods, the collapse of empires — ACP believes every citizen of India deserves that same unbreakable resilience: the power to survive, grow, and lead with dignity. We are not here to protect corrupt systems. We are here to rebuild the nation from the ground up.
The cockroach is nature's ultimate survivor. It represents:
  • Survival against all odds
  • Fearlessness in the face of power
  • Adaptability to any environment
  • Unity — a swarm is unstoppable
  • Never giving up, no matter what
In a political landscape full of hollow symbols, we chose an honest one. The common people of India are like the cockroach — underestimated, resilient, and ultimately impossible to exterminate.
It is our long-term vision horizon. ACP recognises that real change — structural, cultural, and administrative — takes time. 2035 is not just a year; it is our commitment to a sustained, disciplined movement that aims to have transformed the political landscape of Delhi and beyond within the next decade. Every campaign, every volunteer, every RTI filed today is a brick in that future.
ACP is currently operating as a grassroots political movement building its base, network, and manifesto. The formal registration process with the Election Commission of India is part of our roadmap. For the latest status updates, contact us at AamCockroachparty@proton.me.

CAMPAIGNS & POLICIES
Survival Instinct is our grassroots mobilisation campaign currently active in 50+ districts. It trains ordinary citizens — farmers, workers, students, homemakers — to become frontline political actors: understanding their rights, filing complaints, engaging local governance, and building ward-level ACP networks. The swarm begins at the ground level.
This campaign focuses on exposing and eliminating redundant bureaucratic layers that drain public resources and block citizen services. We file RTIs (Right to Information requests), demand digital public ledgers, audit government spending records, and publicly challenge systemic inefficiencies. So far, our volunteers have filed 2,400+ RTIs nationwide.
ACP's seven pillars are:
  • Clean Governance — zero tolerance for corruption, transparent digital systems
  • Honest Leadership — no dynasties, ground-up accountable representation
  • Public Safety — justice for every citizen, not just the powerful
  • Environmental Protection — rivers, air, and soil as national assets
  • Accountability — every rupee tracked, every promise audited
  • National Discipline — civic duty, work ethic, collective responsibility
  • Transparent Administration — open data, open records, open government
