PRIVACY POLICY

We believe transparency is not optional — it's a founding principle. This policy explains exactly how we handle your data.

Last Updated: January 2025 · AamCockroachParty.org

1. WHO WE ARE

The Aam Cockroach Party (ACP) is a political movement operating at AamCockroachParty.org. We are committed to honest, transparent governance — and that starts with how we handle your personal information. For any data-related queries, contact us at AamCockroachparty@proton.me.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We only collect what is necessary. When you use our contact or enlistment form, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your selected role (Volunteer, Donate, Spread the Word, Run for Office)
  • Any message you voluntarily provide

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or sensitive personal data through this website.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Information you submit is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enlistment or inquiry
  • Coordinate volunteer activities and campaign updates
  • Send you relevant ACP communications (you may opt out at any time)
  • Improve our outreach and movement operations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties — ever.

4. EMAIL & COMMUNICATIONS

Our primary contact channel is AamCockroachparty@proton.me, hosted on Proton Mail — an end-to-end encrypted, privacy-first email service. Submissions via our website form open your local mail client and are sent directly to this address. We do not use marketing automation platforms that track email opens or clicks without consent.

5. COOKIES & TRACKING

Our website uses no tracking cookies, no analytics scripts, no advertising pixels, and no third-party trackers. We believe your browsing behaviour is yours alone. The only external resources loaded are:

  • Tailwind CSS (via CDN — no cookies)
  • Google Fonts (font delivery only)

6. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY

Form submissions are delivered directly to our encrypted Proton Mail inbox. We do not store your data on external servers or databases. Access to received enquiries is restricted to authorised ACP personnel only. We apply reasonable technical safeguards to protect any information in our possession.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint if you believe your data has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, email us at AamCockroachparty@proton.me with the subject line "Data Request".

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our site, please contact us immediately for removal.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy as our operations evolve. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. CONTACT US

For any privacy-related concerns, data requests, or questions about this policy:

  • Email: AamCockroachparty@proton.me
  • Website: AamCockroachParty.org
  • Response time: Within 5 business days
